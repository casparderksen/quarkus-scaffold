# Consumer-Driven Contract

A consumer-driven contract is one defined from the consumer's perspective,
expressing exactly what the consumer needs from a provider.

## Why

By letting consumers state their real expectations, the provider learns what
is actually depended upon and can verify against those needs, so it changes
freely as long as it keeps meeting them. The alternative is a provider-driven
approach, where a published specification is the single authority and
consumers stay within it.

## Contrast

In a provider-driven approach the provider's published specification is
authoritative; a consumer-driven contract inverts that origin.
