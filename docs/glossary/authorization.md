# Authorization

Authorization is the verification of permissions — deciding what an already
identified caller is allowed to do.

## Why

Knowing who someone is does not say what they may access. Authorization
applies the rules that grant or deny each action, and it always follows
authentication, since there is nothing to authorize until identity is known.

## Contrast

[Authentication](authentication.md) establishes identity; authorization acts
on it.
