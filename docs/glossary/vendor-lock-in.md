# Vendor Lock-In

Vendor lock-in is excessive dependence on a specific vendor's proprietary
features, making it costly to switch away.

## Why it is bad

The more deeply proprietary capabilities are woven through a system, the more
it is hostage to that vendor's pricing, roadmap, and availability, and the
larger the rewrite required to leave. Isolating vendor specifics behind ports
and adapters is how the risk is contained.
