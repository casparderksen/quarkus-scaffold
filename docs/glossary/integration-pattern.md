# Integration Pattern

An integration pattern is a recognized, reusable approach to connecting
separate systems so they can exchange data or coordinate work.

## Why

Systems are integrated over and over in similar ways, and naming those ways —
synchronous request/response, asynchronous messaging, shared contracts,
translation at the boundary — lets teams reach for a proven approach and
discuss trade-offs in shared terms rather than reinventing each connection.
