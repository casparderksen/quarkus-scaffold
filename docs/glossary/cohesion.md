# Cohesion

Cohesion is the degree to which the responsibilities within a single
component belong together and serve one clear purpose.

## Why

High cohesion makes a component easier to name, understand, change, and
reuse, because everything inside it is there for the same reason. Low
cohesion — a grab-bag of unrelated behavior — forces a reader to hold
several unrelated ideas in mind at once and invites accidental coupling
between them.

## How

Cohesion follows from encapsulating related data with the behavior that
operates on it, and from giving each unit a single reason to change.
