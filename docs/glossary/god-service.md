# God Service

A god service is an oversized service that has accumulated many unrelated
responsibilities into one place.

## Why it is bad

Because it does too much, everything depends on it and it depends on
everything, so it becomes a change and contention bottleneck that is hard to
understand, test, or modify safely. It is the opposite of high cohesion and a
sign that boundaries were never drawn.
