# OAuth 2.0

OAuth 2.0 is a protocol for delegated authorization — letting a user grant an
application limited access to their resources without sharing their
credentials.

## Why

Rather than handing over a password, a user authorizes an application to act
on their behalf within defined scopes, and can revoke that grant later. This
confines what a third party can do and keeps credentials out of its hands.

## Contrast

OAuth 2.0 delegates access; [OpenID Connect](openid-connect.md) adds identity
verification on top of it.
