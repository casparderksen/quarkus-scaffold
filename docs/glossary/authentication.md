# Authentication

Authentication is the verification of identity — establishing who the caller
actually is.

## Why

Every access decision rests on first knowing who is asking. Authentication
answers that question, by checking evidence such as a password, a token, or a
certificate, before any question of permission arises.

## Contrast

Authentication establishes who you are; [authorization](authorization.md)
decides what you may do.
