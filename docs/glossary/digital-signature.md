# Digital Signature

A digital signature is a cryptographic value that lets a recipient verify a
message's integrity and its origin.

## Why

By signing with a private key, a sender produces a value that anyone holding
the matching public key can verify. A valid signature proves the message was
not altered and that it came from the holder of the private key, giving both
integrity and authenticity.
