# Certificate

A certificate is a digitally signed document that binds a public key to an
identity, issued by a trusted authority.

## Why

On its own a public key says nothing about who owns it. A certificate has an
authority attest to that binding with its signature, so a relying party who
trusts the authority can trust that the key belongs to the named identity.
