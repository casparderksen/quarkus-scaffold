# MFA / 2FA

Multi-factor authentication requires more than one independent piece of
evidence to prove identity; two-factor authentication is the common case of
exactly two.

## Why

Any single factor can be compromised — a password guessed, a phone stolen.
Requiring factors of different kinds, such as something known, something held,
and something inherent, means an attacker must defeat several at once, which
sharply raises the bar over a password alone.
