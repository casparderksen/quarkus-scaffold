# Golden Hammer

The golden hammer is over-applying a familiar technology or pattern to
problems it does not fit, because it is the tool one knows best.

## Why it is bad

"When all you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail." Forcing a
favored solution onto ill-suited problems produces awkward, over-complicated
designs and blinds a team to approaches that would fit better. Familiarity, not
suitability, drives the choice.
