# Metrics

Metrics are numerical measurements of a system's behavior over time, such as
latency, throughput, and error rate.

## Why

Aggregated numbers give a compact, continuous picture of health that logs and
traces cannot: they reveal trends, feed alerts when a threshold is crossed,
and quantify how the system behaves under load. They tell you that something
is wrong and how much, which traces and logs then help explain.
